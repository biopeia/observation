# Study index

**Authority:** the single place to resolve what a study, gate or code refers
to. It does not hold status, results or approval: design status lives in
[`docs/design/register.md`](../../docs/design/register.md), evidence status in
[`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md), each result in its own record.

## Naming rule

1. **One canonical key per study**: `<area>.<descriptive-name>[-v<N>]`, the
   `id` of its `experiment.toml`. The name says what is studied; it carries no
   sequence number or letter code.
2. **Parts of a study are named with words**: `<key>/arm-off`,
   `<key>/gate-reuse-closure`, `<key>/development-run` — not `E6`, `D1` or
   `BD-1 OFF`.
3. **Short codes are local.** A document may define codes for its own
   sections, arms or findings if it lists them in a table near the top
   (code → name). A code is never used in another document: a cross-reference
   gives the canonical key or the document title.
4. **Frozen evidence is not rewritten.** Existing protocols, results and
   audits keep the codes they were written with. [Code meanings](#code-meanings)
   translates them.
5. New documents follow rules 1–3 from 2026-10-04.

Adopted by owner decision on 2026-10-04.
